  - propertyValue: The Addgene Terms of Use specifies under what legal requirements Addgene data may be accessed. Data is available for use for non-commercial purposes, but users must get express written consent of Addgene to exploit the data for commercial purposes. All copyright, trademark and other proprietary notices must be retained on the data. As consent must be aquired for commercial use, this license only allows non-commercial use (a separate agreement must be entered into for commercial use).
